About Cars on Rent

Your Journey Across Northeast India Starts Here.

Cars on Rent provides convenient car rental and travel solutions from Guwahati for travellers exploring Assam and the Northeastern states of India — whether it's a short city trip, an airport transfer or a longer scenic tour.

We focus on comfortable travel, easy booking and journeys planned around where you actually want to go. Families, business travellers and tourists can pick a vehicle that fits the trip, and we help with the rest.
